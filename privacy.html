Privacy policy: what TRWS Studio reads, keeps and shares

Last updated 27 October 2026. TRWS Studio is run by Pax et Lumen Studio for the YouTube channel The Reason Why.

YouTube API Services

TRWS Studio uses the YouTube API Services. By commenting on the channel's videos you're using YouTube, and the YouTube Terms of Service apply. Google's handling of your data is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

What it reads

  • Public comments on The Reason Why's own videos: the comment text, the commenter's public channel name, the time, and the like count.
  • The channel's own account, through the access the channel owner grants, so it can post replies and comments as The Reason Why.

It doesn't read private messages, email, viewing history, or anything outside the channel's own videos.

How it uses that data

  • To draft and post one reply as the channel, and to avoid replying to the same person too often.
  • To check each draft against the channel's comment rules before it posts.

To draft and check replies, the comment text is sent to two AI services: Anthropic (Claude) and Groq. Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising.

What it keeps

A log of comments and the channel's replies, kept on the studio's own computer. It's kept so the channel doesn't answer the same comment twice. Ask us and we'll delete your entries.

Your choices

  • To remove your comments or log entries, email paxetlumenstudio@gmail.com.
  • The channel owner can revoke the tool's access at any time on Google's third-party access page.
