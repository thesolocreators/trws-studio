Faster answers for every viewer of The Reason Why

TRWS Studio is the comment tool behind The Reason Why, the YouTube channel about why everyday things exist.

What it does

It reads the comments on the channel's own videos and answers them as the channel, through the YouTube API Services. Every reply is checked against the channel's comment rules before it posts. When a video goes live, it also posts one bonus fact under it, once the channel owner approves the text.

Who uses it

Only the channel's own team. It isn't a public app.
